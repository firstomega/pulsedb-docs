# Privacy policy

<p class="meta">Effective date: November 17, 2026</p>

This policy explains how **Sentire Labs LLC**, a New Jersey limited liability company ("Sentire Labs", "we", "us"), handles information in connection with **PulseDB**, an app for Atlassian Confluence Cloud sold through the Atlassian Marketplace, and with this documentation website.

## 1. The short version

- PulseDB runs entirely on Atlassian's platform. **We never receive, see, or store the contents of your Confluence site**, including your databases.
- PulseDB uses no analytics, tracking, cookies, or outside services.
- We only receive personal information when you contact us, and, as a Marketplace seller, the limited purchase and licensing details Atlassian shares with us.

## 2. Information PulseDB processes inside your Confluence site

When a person views a page with a PulseDB chart, PulseDB reads the relevant database's columns and rows, and the display names of people in person columns, **on behalf of that person and only to the extent they have access**. This happens inside Atlassian's Forge platform, in memory, to calculate and draw the chart. The information is not stored by PulseDB and is not sent to Sentire Labs or anyone else.

Chart settings (such as the chosen database and columns) are saved by Confluence as part of the page, under your Confluence site's own controls.

If a chart fails to load unexpectedly, a short technical error message (without database contents) may be written to app logs on Atlassian's platform. Atlassian keeps these logs for a limited period. We may view these logs only to diagnose and fix problems.

## 3. Information we receive directly

| Information | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, email address, and anything you include in your message | When you email support | To answer you and fix problems |
| Licensing and billing contact details for your organization (such as company name, technical and billing contact names and emails, license type, and site address) | When your organization installs, trials, or buys PulseDB through the Atlassian Marketplace | To manage licenses, provide support, and meet legal and tax obligations |

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. This website

This documentation site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add cookies, analytics, or tracking to it. GitHub may collect technical information (such as IP addresses) to operate and secure its service, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

## 5. Service providers

| Provider | Role |
|---|---|
| Atlassian | Hosts and runs PulseDB (Forge), sells and bills licenses (Marketplace) |
| GitHub | Hosts this documentation website |
| Our email provider | Delivers and stores support emails |

## 6. How long we keep information

- Support emails: up to 3 years after the conversation ends, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
- Licensing and billing records: as long as required for accounting and tax purposes.
- PulseDB keeps no copy of your Confluence data, so there is nothing to delete on our side when you uninstall.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email **support@sentire-labs.com**. We will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Information about your Confluence site itself is controlled by your organization and by Atlassian. Please contact your Confluence administrator for those requests.

## 8. Security

PulseDB runs only on Atlassian's infrastructure, makes no outside connections, and asks for read-only permissions. See [Data and security]({{ '/security/' | relative_url }}) for details.

## 9. International transfers

Sentire Labs is based in the United States. Information we receive directly (such as support emails) is processed in the United States. PulseDB's processing of your Confluence data happens on Atlassian's platform, under Atlassian's data residency settings for your site.

## 10. Children

PulseDB is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.

## 11. Changes

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date. For significant changes, we will also update the note on our Marketplace listing.
