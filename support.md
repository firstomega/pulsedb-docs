# Support

<p class="lede">We're happy to help.</p>

## Contact

Email **[support@sentire-labs.com](mailto:support@sentire-labs.com)**.

We aim to reply within **2 business days** (Monday to Friday, US Eastern time).

To help us answer quickly, please include:

- your Confluence site address (for example, `your-company.atlassian.net`),
- what you expected to see and what you saw instead,
- a screenshot of the chart and of its settings, if possible,
- the message shown on the chart, if any.

Please don't send us exports of your database or other sensitive content. We don't need them to help.

## Before you write

Most questions are answered in the [User guide]({{ '/user-guide/' | relative_url }}), especially the **Troubleshooting** section.

## Security reports

Email **support@sentire-labs.com** with the subject "Security". See [Data and security]({{ '/security/' | relative_url }}).

## Feature requests

Tell us what you'd like PulseDB to do next. We read every request.
